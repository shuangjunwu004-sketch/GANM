Our mission

Global AI Nexus at Michigan explores and advances artificial intelligence and its interdisciplinary applications. We connect students across schools, and we connect the university with industry.

Cross-disciplinary collaboration

We bring together students from the College of Engineering, LSA, the Ross School of Business, and every other U-M school and college.

Innovation with hard tech

We pair AI with emerging and hard technologies to create research, educational and entrepreneurial opportunities for students.

Academia meets industry

Speaker events, workshops, knowledge sharing and professional networking, all designed around students.

A global community

An inclusive, open community that values diversity, authenticity and collaboration across academic, professional and cultural backgrounds.

What we do

Members join at least one event, meeting or team project each semester. Here is what that looks like.

Talks & panels

Invited speakers from research labs and industry on where AI is heading.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions on tools and methods, open to beginners and experts alike.

Student projects

Interdisciplinary teams working on AI agents, robotics, AI in education and more.

Networking

Connections with U-M faculty, mentors, student groups and partner organizations.
