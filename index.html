Our mission

Global AI Alliance at Umich explores and advances artificial intelligence and its interdisciplinary applications. We connect students across schools, and we connect the university with industry.

Cross-disciplinary collaboration

We bring together students from the College of Engineering, LSA, the Ross School of Business, and every other U-M school and college.

Innovation with hard tech

We pair AI with emerging and hard technologies to create research, educational and entrepreneurial opportunities for students.

Academia meets industry

Speaker events, workshops, knowledge sharing and professional networking, all designed around students.

A global community

An inclusive, open community that values diversity, authenticity and collaboration across academic, professional and cultural backgrounds.

What we do

Members join at least one event, meeting or team project each semester. Here is what that looks like.

Talks & panels

Invited speakers from research labs and industry on where AI is heading.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions on tools and methods, open to beginners and experts alike.

Student projects

Interdisciplinary teams working on AI agents, robotics, AI in education and more.

Networking

Connections with U-M faculty, mentors, student groups and partner organizations.

How to apply

  1. Complete the online form: Provide your basic background, academic program, and indicate up to two roles you are most excited about.
  2. Share your statement of intent: In 150–250 words, tell us why you want to join GAAM, your interests in AI or interdisciplinary tech, and what initiatives you would like to initiate.
  3. Casual coffee chat: Selected applicants will be invited for a 15-minute informal conversation to discuss mutual fit, team culture, and ideas for the upcoming year.
What we value

We look for curiosity, proactive ownership, and a collaborative mindset. No prior deep learning or engineering background is required for operational or community roles.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so apply early.
